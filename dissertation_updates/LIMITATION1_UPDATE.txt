SECTION 1.6 – LIMITATION 1 (revised)

Limitation 1: Analytical Representation of the Nigerian Operational Context and Use of Real Facility Data

The study relies primarily on publicly available benchmark datasets augmented with physics-informed Nigerian-specific perturbations, together with the real Petrobras 3W Dataset 2.0.0 (Vargas et al., 2019; 3W Dataset 2.0.0). The 3W corpus supplies genuine offshore oil-well telemetry and rare undesirable events from operational Petrobras facilities, thereby providing a real-facility target domain against which domain-adaptation performance is measured. This partially closes the ecological-validity gap that would exist if only simulated or laboratory data were used. However, the 3W wells operate under Brazilian offshore conditions; they do not fully replicate the specific combination of high humidity, intermittent power, and sabotage-related stressors characteristic of the Niger Delta. Full live Nigerian refinery and pipeline telemetry remains the principal direction for future research (Chapter Five, Section 5.5). The physics-informed augmentation pipeline described in Chapter Three, Section 3.4.3, continues to supply the Nigerian-specific environmental and operational stressors applied to the international benchmarks.
